Monkey D. Luffy

Monkey D. Luffy is the main protagonist of the manga and anime series One Piece. He is a cheerful, determined pirate who dreams of becoming the Pirate King by finding the legendary treasure known as the One Piece. After accidentally eating the Gum-Gum Fruit (later revealed to have a deeper significance), Luffy gained the ability to stretch his body like rubber. As the captain of the Straw Hat Pirates, Luffy travels across the seas, gathering loyal crewmates, challenging powerful enemies, and fighting against injustice. He is known for his unwavering loyalty to friends, fearless nature, and strong sense of freedom. His adventures have made him one of the most iconic characters in anime and manga.
